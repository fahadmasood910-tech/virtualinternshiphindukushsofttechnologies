Day15:

Aaj humne University of Chitral kee official website ka ek simple clone design kiiya using HTML and  CSS. Sabse pehle maine ek clean header banaya jisme left side paar university ka logo orr right side par quick links rakhen jaiise Email, Jobs orr Tenders. Header koo flexbox kii madad se proper alignment diya taake woh neat and organized lagee. Uske baad maine navigation bar design kiiya jisme Home, About, Admissions, Academics, Research orr Contact kee links add kiyee. Navigation ko purple background aur hover underline effect diya joo website ko ek professional look detaa haii.
Phir maine ek  admission section banaya jiisme admission ka banner image add kiya jiase ke uni ke website par mojud haii . Isko maine full width dia  . Homepage ke content section me maine alag alag cards design kiye jiisme Welcome Message, VC Message, Latest News and Upcoming Events show kiyaa . Cards ko border, spacing aur simple styling ke saath design kiya taaake information clear orr  readable laage.
End mee maine ek footer banaya joo poore lower part ko cover karta haii. Footer me university ka address, phone number aur extra links add kiaa jaise About, Contact orr Privacy Policy. Isko bhii purple background ke saath full width banaya.
Is task see maine seekha kee header orr navigation bar koo flexbox ke zariye kaise design kiya jata haii,  content ko cards layout me kaise organize karte haiin orr footer ko ek professional look kaise dete haiin. 
Is clone website ko banane ka lia maine real University website ko kafi gour se study kia orr uske ka bad ya clone banay orr ajj ke task ka lia maine kesi bhi internet orr youtube video ka sahara nahi lia . Maine is clone as simple as possible banaya takee main css or hmtl ke concept ko waqi me use kar sakun .
